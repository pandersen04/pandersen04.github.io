New Year New You!

Paige's guide to making 2026 your year

This year I decided that I wanted to make a change, its been almost 2 months and I have felt amazing. Here are some of the dos and don'ts I made for myself at the beginning of the year and hopefully this can help you if you are feeling like your life is not in your control

Do-s

  • Wake up 30 minutes earlier - but make sure you get 8 hours of sleep
  • Make your bed everyday
  • Workout at least 2x a week
  • Prioritze whole foods
  • Spend time with the people who want to see you succeed
  • Start saving, use a monthly budget template

Don't-s

  • Immediatly go on your phone in the morning
  • Say "i'll do it later"
  • Make excuses to not do something
  • Spend time with people who wouldn't text you asking if you got home safe
  • Go get fast food because it's cheaper in the moment
  • Schedule everything, go with the flow

One thing that I always lacked was getting up and going in the morning, this would lead to me constantly being late and feeling like I wasted my mornings. I started to follow a morning routine and it has absolutely changed the trajectory of my days. People underestimate the power of a good morning routine!

Example of a good morning routine:

  1. Wake up to alarm
  2. Wash face and brush teeth
  3. 5-10 minute stretch
  4. Shower
  5. Get dressed
  6. Do hair and makeup - if wanted
  7. Eat breakfast and drink coffee if desired
  8. Pack bag / prep for the day
