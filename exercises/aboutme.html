About Paige

Hi there! I am the face behind the lense- Paige! I am 21 years old, and I from a small town called Lakeville, Massachusetts. Fun fact about me- I was actually born in Rhode Island!

I am a senior Social Media Marketing major as well as a Theater major at the University of Massachusetts- Amherst! I started my photography journey 2 years ago, and since then I haven't stopped! I definetly have a ways to go, but I am so excited to start to get my work out there.

Some things that I love are: my family, my boyfriend, my friends, music, travelling, and of course- coffee! You can almost always find me with a coffee in my hand.

I am graduating from UMass Amherst in Spring 2026 so I will officially have nothing else to do and I will be free for WHENEVER you need!

I love it when someone is comfortable in front of the lense, its something that I had always struggled with growing up and something that I strive to help other with. I firmly believe that you can tell from someone's smile if they want to be there or not!

One thing about me is that I can talk to anyone- and I mean anyone. If we get to talking you better buckle up! We will be friends by the end of the conversation :)
