Help! I want to move out but I don't know how!

Here's Paige's list of dos and don'ts for moving out

As my time as a licensed real estate agent, here are some tips that i've heard for when you are thinking about moving out

Do-s

  • Decide what area you want to look in
  • Make a timeline of when you would want to move
  • Check your credit score
  • Start saving boxes- Amazon, Target, any big box that gets shipped to you
  • Talk with a real estate agent
  • Set your price range based on how much you can afford

Don't-s

  • Immediatly list your home for sale or break your lease
  • handle everything by yourself
  • look for places outside of your budget
  • consider places extremely far away
  • Start packing up your home immediately

One thing people underestimate is how much time it takes for a home to be sold or how hard it is to find your perfect home in the market now

Here's my recommendation if you are looking to move out

  1. Talk with a real estate agent
  2. Establish your price range based off what you can afford
  3. Consider how far away you are looking to move
  4. Start going to open houses- you don't have to buy the first one you see
  5. Start decluttering your home- what don't you want to take?
  6. Remind yourself its okay to be picky, but things can also be changed later on
  7. Don't rush the process
  8. Take it one step at a time with the help of your real estate agent!
