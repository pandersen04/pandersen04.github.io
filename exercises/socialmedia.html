Hi there!

Like every young person I have quite a bit of social media experience

But I wanted to do a little show and tell on this page and walk you through what my social media expeirence has been!

So, lets start! I began taking social media more seriously in my senior year of high school. I was a "prom dress influencer" for my local prom dress boutique. While being an influencer there every week I would go in and film/take content and post weekly. Thats when I realized that I enjoyed social media- and I wasn't too bad at it either. A couple of my posts got popular (500-600 likes sometimes!) and not long after the prom dress season was over.

Then, I went to college. I kept up with the "influencer" look on my instagram page for a while, especially when I went to college, but I never really took social media seriously again. Until, my sophomore year of college. I was working on creating my own major in Real Estate Marketing- which was really me taking classes that would help me be a real estate agent. Although a lot of people thought I would be marketing for only real estate agents....

During spring semester of my sophomore year at UMass I took a class called Influencer Marketing- which remains to be my favorite class that I have taken! Everything came very naturally for me and I found myself wondering more and more about social media. Then! After a year or two of explaining what my major was, I had an epiphany fall of my senior year, all I did in all of my classes for my major was talk about- you guessed it- social media! I then decided to change my major to Social Media Marketing, after that people didn't need much of an explanation of what my major is!

Not long after I changed the title of my major, I interviewed and got chosen for an internship as a social media manager for the BDIC careers instagram page. While interning I created instagram posts and reels and would consistently post 2-3x a week in addiiton to collaborating with other school instagram accounts. Now, as I am writing this I am graduating in less than a month! My plans for the summer are to help my mom (who is a real estate agent) run her social media account while I start to build my real estate business!
